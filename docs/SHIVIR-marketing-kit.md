# SHIVIR Marketing Kit (draft v1)

Marketing content only — **none of this is in the app**. Uzbek-first, Russian where it helps.
**Status: draft. Have 2–3 native Uzbek (and Russian) speakers read it before publishing** — the
wording is unreviewed.

## Ground rules (from SHIVIR's own principles)

1. **Examples are examples.** Every sample message below is illustrative. In any post or ad, label
   it "Namuna" / "Пример". Never present one as a real message from a real person, and never invent
   activity ("500 kishi yozdi…").
2. **Kind by default.** Prompts invite compliments, honesty, questions, memories. No "roast me",
   no "who has a crush on you", no dares, no pressure to expose or shame anyone.
3. **No sender hints.** Never tease "guess who wrote this". The promise is: *they can't know.*
4. **One promise, said plainly:** *Shivir o'zi xabar to'qimaydi.* Only real messages arrive.
5. **Honest anonymity line:** "Ular kim yozganini bilishmaydi." (true). Don't say nobody could ever
   find out — the privacy page says what is hashed and why.

## Age-policy hold (read before publishing anything)

SHIVIR has **no age policy yet** and the product states no audience or age anywhere. Until the
founder/legal decision is made, **do not run campaigns aimed at school-age or age-sensitive audiences.**

## 8. Content formats that fit the product (no new features needed)

- **Story sticker + link in bio** — creator posts a prompt (§4), replies with the SHIVIR card of the
  best message. *The card has no link by design — put the link in the caption.*
- **"Bugungi eng iliq xabar"** — creators (with the sender's non-identifying text only) share their
  own received message as a card.
- **Group/Community prompt** — a team or club shares one prompt and one link *per person*. (⏸ HOLD: school classes.)
- **Weekly rhythm (organic):** Mon "Maslahat", Wed "Rahmat", Fri "Halol fikr", weekend "Xotira".
- **One dense community first:** pick a single tight adult group (one football
  supporters' chat, one creator circle, one workplace team; ⏸ HOLD: university/school groups) and seed the first 30–50 links there before going wide.

## 9. What not to publish

- Fake or "recreated" screenshots of real chats. Fake counters ("1,203 messages today").
- "Find out who sent it", "reveal", "hint" mechanics of any kind.
- Anything aimed at minors' embarrassment, rankings of people, or dares.
- Any claim stronger than the privacy page. When in doubt, link to it.
